The Maison

Composed in darkness,
worn in moonlight.

Ambre Noir began in a single candlelit room in old Abuja, where perfumer Ade recalled the night markets of his childhood — smoke, resin, roses pressed between pages.

We press, macerate and bottle in small batches. No colouring, no photostability filters, no shortcuts. A shade over a thousand bottles leave the studio each season.
